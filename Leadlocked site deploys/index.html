FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need a new website?+
No. We give you a form link to add to your existing site, Google Business page, Facebook page, or even run directly in ads. If your site has a contact section, it works.
What is the difference between Basic and Pro?+
Basic sends an AI-written reply to every new lead and notifies you by email. Pro does all that plus three exclusive features: instant SMS alerts to your phone the moment a lead comes in, replies timed to land during business hours so they look like a real person sent them, and up to 3 automatic follow-up emails if the lead doesn't respond. All three features are Pro only.
Why would I want replies delayed? Won't faster always win?+
A reply arriving at 2 AM on a Sunday screams automated. Pro plan replies are timed to land during normal business hours so the lead thinks a real person on your team responded. That builds more trust and gets more replies back than an obviously robotic response.
Will the replies sound like my business?+
Yes. When you sign up you provide your business name, trade, and service area. The AI uses that information to write replies that sound like they came from you or someone on your team — professional, personal, and never mentioning AI.
What happens when a lead replies?+
Their reply is forwarded directly to your email. Pro users also get an instant SMS notification so you know the moment someone responds. The automatic follow-up sequence stops immediately the moment the lead replies — no duplicate messages.
How long does setup take?+
Most contractors are live within 1-2 hours. We send you everything you need after checkout — your form link, setup instructions, and we get your business info configured on our end.
Can I cancel anytime?+
Absolutely. Click the manage billing link in your welcome email, log into the customer portal and cancel with one click. Or click the link below. No phone calls, no hassle.
