SMS Opt-In

This page documents how LeadLocked collects SMS consent from contractors during signup.

For A2P 10DLC Reviewers: This page shows the SMS opt-in consent flow used by LeadLocked. Contractors provide their phone number and explicitly check a consent box during the onboarding process after subscribing at leadlocked.ai via Stripe checkout. The consent language, privacy policy, and terms are displayed at the time of opt-in.
Program Details
Program Name: LeadLocked SMS Notifications
Sender: LeadLocked (leadlocked.ai)
Message Type: Account notifications — new lead alerts and lead reply alerts sent to subscribed contractors
Frequency: Varies based on lead volume. One SMS per new lead, one SMS per lead reply.
Opt-Out: Reply STOP to any message
Help: Reply HELP or email support@leadlocked.ai
Rates: Message and data rates may apply
